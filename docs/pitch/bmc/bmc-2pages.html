The model in one sentence. The apps are free for everyone who sends an alert and, during the pilot, for the emergency services that receive it. Money comes from paid hardware (an OBD dongle and the Sellekni Box) for drivers and fleets who want stronger crash detection, then from institutional licences and insurers, with non-dilutive funding to start. Payers and prices are still to be validated.

2 · Value proposition

What problem do we solve?

  • Victim: if you can’t call, your phone alerts with your exact position. 30 s to say “I’m OK”, then it sends itself. Works by plain SMS when data is weak; queues when there is no signal.
  • Witness: report in 5 taps in the app, or 4 questions from a web link; no account.
  • 14 operator: one merged, located report with an estimated risk and its reasons; hospitals ranked by capability and travel time. The human decides; every action is logged.
  • Hospital ED: a pre-alert before the patient arrives, acknowledged.
  • Hardware buyer: the OBD dongle sharpens the speed drop; the Box’s ±200 g sensor sees the real crash pulse that phones clip, with its own GNSS, LTE and backup power.
  • We don’t replace 14 / 1021, diagnose, or claim real-time bed data.

Why hardware

What a phone can’t see

  • Phones saturate at about ±8–16 g, so a hard crash is clipped.
  • Sellekni Box: a ±200 g sensor sees the real crash pulse; own GNSS, LTE and backup power, so it works even if the phone is thrown or broken.
  • OBD dongle: the car’s own speed, faster than 1 Hz GPS.
  • Business: the app stays free. Drivers and fleets buy the dongle (entry) or the box (premium, optional SIM subscription). No price set yet.
2 · Schema · Sellekni Box (ESP32)

Reference design and firmware flow

  • Indicative parts (none validated, no prices): ESP32-class module, ADXL375-class ±200 g accelerometer, 6-axis IMU, LTE modem with GNSS and antennas, nano-SIM holder, 12/24 V automotive buck with surge protection, supercap or small cell, optional CAN transceiver, enclosure.
  • Firmware: the same detector rules as the phone; the packet encoder ported to C. Acceptance test: our 302 valid and 134 invalid test packets, byte for byte.
  • Network: LTE, because Algeria plans to retire 2G/3G by 2029; check the module’s bands against the operators’ LTE bands before buying.
  • Before sale: homologation under Executive Decree 26-97, GSMA TAC for the cellular module, automotive EMC tests, warranty and liability.
3 · Schema · OBD link

What the car’s diagnostic port adds

It can:
  • vehicle speed: Mode 01, PID 0D, in km/h
  • engine speed: PID 0C
  • faster speed readings than 1 Hz GPS
It can’t:
  • read “airbag deployed”: no standard signal, it depends on the manufacturer
  • B0001 is an airbag-circuit fault code, not a deployment
