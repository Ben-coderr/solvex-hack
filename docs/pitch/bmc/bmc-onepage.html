SolveX 3.0 · Challenge 1 · Road emergencies in Algeria · A phone-based crash alert that reaches the emergency operations room by internet or plain SMS, with an estimated risk and a hospital suggestion.

Team: Cheikh Mohamed Tahar · Benkorich Abdenour · Ould Miloud Zakaria · Bouzid Mustapha · Baghdad Imad Eddine 31 Oct 2026 · prototype, synthetic data   Verified Hypothesis Paid

The model in one sentence. The apps are free for everyone who sends an alert and, during the pilot, for the emergency services that receive it. Money comes from paid hardware (an OBD dongle and the Sellekni Box) for drivers and fleets who want stronger crash detection, then from institutional licences and insurers, with non-dilutive funding to start. Payers and prices are still to be validated.

2 · Value proposition

What problem do we solve?

  • Victim: if you can’t call, your phone alerts with your exact position. 30 s to say “I’m OK”, then it sends itself. Works by plain SMS when data is weak; queues when there is no signal.
  • Witness: report in 5 taps in the app, or 4 questions from a web link; no account.
  • 14 operator: one merged, located report with an estimated risk and its reasons; hospitals ranked by capability and travel time. The human decides; every action is logged.
  • Hospital ED: a pre-alert before the patient arrives, acknowledged.
  • Hardware buyer: the OBD dongle sharpens the speed drop; the Box’s ±200 g sensor sees the real crash pulse that phones clip, with its own GNSS, LTE and backup power.
  • We don’t replace 14 / 1021, diagnose, or claim real-time bed data.

1 · Customer segments

Who are they?

  • Free users: drivers and passengers with Android phones (≈ 87 % of phones in Algeria) StatCounter; witnesses
  • Free receivers (pilot): Civil Protection 14 / 1021, Gendarmerie 1055, Police 17, SAMU where present; hospital EDs
  • Hardware buyers: drivers who want stronger detection; fleets and transport operators (buses, coaches, lorries) H

  • Pilot early adopters: one wilaya’s ops room, one university hospital, one fleet
9 · Cost structure

What will it cost?

  • Fixed: team time (the main cost); hosting in Algeria; map and routing hosting
  • Per incident: about one SMS receipt and one hospital pre-alert, plus gateway SIMs
  • Hardware: parts (ESP32, ±200 g sensor, LTE/GNSS modem, power), enclosure, box SIM data, homologation (Executive Decree 26-97), warranty
  • One-off: medical and legal review, trademark (INAPI), test phones
  • No supplier quotes yet, so no figures H
5 · Revenue streams

How will it be funded?

  • Apps: always free for citizens. Nobody pays to be alerted.
  • Paid OBD dongle: entry-level hardware sale to drivers and fleets
  • Paid Sellekni Box: premium hardware sale, with an optional connectivity subscription for its SIM H
  • B2G: licence and support contract for the ops-room software H
  • Fleets and insurers: crash notification and data export subscription H
  • Now: prizes, incubators, Algeria Startup Fund after labelling Route verified
  • No price set before interviewing 3 fleet managers and 2 insurers.
