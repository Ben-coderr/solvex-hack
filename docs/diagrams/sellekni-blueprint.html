Phone sensing

docs/13

A phone accelerometer usually tops out between ±8 g and ±16 g. A full frontal test at 56 km/h produces 36 to 46 g for about 100 ms (Locey et al., 2012). So the phone cannot report how hard the crash was. It can report how long the sensor stayed pinned at its ceiling, and GPS can report the speed collapse. The Realme’s own range is measured in test T1.

Realme 10 Pro+ field plan

Known from the spec sheet

  • Launched with Android 13 and Realme UI 4.0. Yours runs Android 14, the Realme UI 5.0 generation (you confirmed it).
  • Accelerometer and gyroscope; no barometer.
  • Dual nano-SIM, so choose which SIM sends the SMS.
  • Realme’s battery management can stop background apps. Sellekni needs auto-launch, foreground and background activity allowed; menu names vary by version.
  • A sideloaded app may have “restricted settings” on SMS; onboarding must detect it and guide the user.

Unknown until measured

  • The accelerometer’s maximum range and resolution (T1).
  • The real sampling rate; the detector needs at least 45 Hz (T3).
  • GPS speed lag; the confirm window assumes about 0.8 s (T5).
  • Whether sensors keep streaming with the screen off (T6).
  • T1Inventoryrange, resolution, minimum delay
  • T2Rest noiseflat for 60 s, noise below 0.5 g
  • T3Real sampling raterequest 50, 100, 200 Hz; median at least 50 Hz
  • T4Saturation probepadded drops, find the true ceiling
  • T5GPS speed lagconstant speed, then one controlled brake
  • T6Screen-off survival30 min, no gap longer than 1 s
  • T7Battery cost60 min, screen dimmed vs foreground service
  • T8Hard negativesbumps, braking, door slam, 5 times each, 0 alerts
  • T9Permission drilla teammate installs it unaided
  • T10Airplane-mode drilldata off, SMS reaches the gateway

Never stage a crash. Real-road tests are negatives and low-speed manoeuvres in a closed, legal area with a passenger holding the phone.

Design Thinking evidence, nothing invented

  • Empathize: five 15-minute conversations: a driver, an operator or volunteer, an ED nurse or doctor, a fleet manager or insurer, optionally a traffic officer. Keep date, role, three quotes and three pain points. No names, no recordings without consent.
  • Test: five people, three tasks (the countdown, reading a new report, reporting as a witness), a log of where they hesitated and what we changed.
  • With 24 hours left and nobody reachable, write “not yet validated” on the slide and run three or four hallway tests at the hackathon, labelled “hackathon participants, not emergency staff”. Honest beats invented.
